HOW TO PLAY

🖥️ DESKTOP CONTROLS

◀ or A Move Left
▶ or D Move Right
SPACE or ▲ / W Jump
▼ or S Roll / Slide Down
ESC or P Pause Game

📱 MOBILE CONTROLS

Swipe ◀ Left / ▶ Right to switch lanes, Swipe ▲ Up to Jump, or use the on-screen touch buttons at the bottom of the screen.

💎 OBJECTIVES & POWER-UPS

💎
Glowing Diamonds: Worth 10 points each. Collect as many as you can!
🧲
Cyber Magnet: Automatically attracts all nearby diamonds for 10 seconds!
🛡️
Energy Shield: Shields Prakash against one obstacle crash!
⚡
Hyper Nitro: Invincible speed boost that blasts through obstacles!
⚠️
Obstacles: Jump over low laser barriers or dodge tall energy monoliths!
